# RWA Lens brand guide

RWA Lens inspects Solana tokens and reports exactly what it can prove. The brand has
the same job: state the thing plainly, and never imply a guarantee the product does
not make. Everything here is regenerated by `node scripts/build-brand-kit.mjs`.

## The mark

A faceted prism. The lit top face is the surface a holder sees; the ruled layers
beneath it are the raw units, the extensions and the authorities the product exposes.
It reads as a lens and as a stack of records at the same time, which is the argument
of the product in one shape.

Keep clear space of at least the height of the top facet on every side. The smallest
sizes are 20px for the mark alone and 120px wide for the lockup. Do not rotate it,
recolour the facet outside the palette below, add effects, or place the navy mark on a
dark ground; use the reverse mark instead.

## Wordmark

`RWA` in roman, `Lens` in italic, then an indigo period. The period is the whole
voice: the sentence ends, the claim is finished, nothing is being oversold. Never set
the wordmark in the UI typeface, never drop the period, and never colour the period
anything but indigo.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | `#101a3a` | Primary ink, mark body, dark grounds |
| Navy soft | `#46516e` | Secondary text |
| Navy faint | `#616b82` | Labels and captions. Smallest passing grey at 9px |
| Indigo | `#5b5ce2` | The period, eyebrows, links, one accent per surface |
| Facet | `#9e9ff8` | The lit face of the mark only |
| Canvas | `#f6f7fb` | Page ground |
| Paper | `#ffffff` | Cards and surfaces |
| Rule | `#e3e6ef` | The ledger grid and hairlines |

Amber `#f59e0b` on `#fff5de`, green `#0f7450` on `#e1f3e9` and red `#b3213c` on
`#fce6ea` carry state in the product. They are never brand colours and never appear in
marketing artwork; in this product a colour that means "attention" has to keep meaning
only that.

Every pairing shipped here meets WCAG AA at its rendered size. If you introduce a new
pairing, measure it rather than trusting the swatch.

## Typography

A serif wordmark against a sans interface, deliberately: the brand speaks, the data
does not. Georgia (falling back to Times New Roman) sets the wordmark. Inter sets
everything else, with `ui-monospace` for addresses, amounts and anything a reader might
compare character by character. Never set an address in a proportional face.

## The ledger field

The faint ruled grid behind the artwork is a ledger, not decoration. Use it at low
contrast, aligned to the composition, and never across the full frame at full strength.
On the profile artwork it is clipped to the rounded corner.

## Voice

Write what was observed. "Verified", "unavailable" and "not verified" are three
different words and none of them is a synonym for the others. Never write copy that
implies RWA Lens signs, custodies, endorses an issuer, or checks compliance; it reads
public state and says what it found. Prefer "read-only" to "safe", and prefer naming a
limit to leaving it implied.

## Files

Marks and wordmarks are SVG. Profiles ship as SVG and PNG at 1024 square. Social,
ad, header and wallpaper artwork ships as PNG at the size named in the filename, each
rendered from the SVG beside it so the raster and vector cannot drift.

SVG wordmarks use live text rather than outlines, so a machine without Georgia will
substitute its fallback. For print or embedding where that matters, render the PNG or
convert the text to outlines first.
