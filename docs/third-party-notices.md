## September 2026 visual redesign

The [RIVR DeFi](https://motionsites.ai/?prompt=rivr-defi-landing),
[Digital Reality](https://motionsites.ai/?prompt=digital-reality-hero), and
[Cinematic Landing Page](https://motionsites.ai/?prompt=cinematic-landing-page)
MotionSites premium templates informed the visual direction. They were accessed
through the user's connected account. The implementation adapts their use of
cinematic imagery, large typography, framed compositions and restrained motion.
It does not redistribute premium prompt text, template video files, remote fonts,
third-party logos or financial claims.

The landscape and portrait optical sculptures were generated for RWA Lens with
the built-in image-generation tool. Typography, optical marks and campaign
compositions are authored in the repository. Wallpaper export dimensions are
upscaled compositions; the original generated artwork is 1672 × 941 landscape
and 941 × 1672 portrait. Editable sources and the Inter license ship with the kit.
