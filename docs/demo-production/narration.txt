A token balance tells you how much you hold. It doesn't tell you the whole story.

R W A Lens brings the details together, so you can understand real-world tokens on Solana before taking the next step.

Let's open the app and look at U S D Y. The inspector reads the mint directly from Solana mainnet. There's no wallet to connect.

Start with Overview to check the full address and token program. Then move between Balances, Controls, Liquidity, and Evidence.

The controls explain who can mint or freeze tokens, alongside restrictions the inspector can observe.

Open Evidence to see when the data was read and the context behind each call.

Then download the observation as JSON or C S V. It's a record you can keep, review, or bring into your own workflow.

Next, open Yield. Filter tokenized-asset pools by issuer, pair, or liquidity, then jump straight from a pool to its token inspection.

Save tokens to your Watchlist to find them again on this device.

Pool activity comes from market-data sources. Historical fees aren't a promise of future returns. Issuer reserve links take you to the issuer's own reports.

That's the idea: a clearer path from discovery to understanding, with the evidence close at hand.
